⛽ Payment Hold

Authorize-and-capture payments on Arkade, gas-pump style: tap to lock a hold, the merchant captures the metered amount, the rest returns automatically. No payment processor, no backend — a covenant VTXO is the escrow and the chain is the webhook.

🧾
Merchant terminal

Announce holds, watch authorizations, capture or void.
👛
Customer wallet

Tap to authorize, watch the settlement, reclaim after the timelock.
🛝
Contract playground

Compile the reference .ark contracts to their JSON artifact in-browser.

Before you start

The arkade regtest stack must be running (arkd :7070, emulator :7073, esplora :3000). Open the two role pages in separate tabs — holds hand off between them automatically. Fund the customer wallet with the pnpm faucet command shown on its page. See the README for the full walkthrough.
